Speaking Topics

Available Presentations

Che-Sun Lee draws on the research and analysis behind The Amero: Digital Bridge to the Fintech Future to deliver presentations that are rigorous, timely, and accessible to diverse audiences, from finance professionals and policymakers to academic conferences and technology forums.

⚖

The Future of Money: Digital Currency, Stablecoins, and Monetary Trust

How digital currencies, programmable money, and stablecoin regulation are fundamentally reshaping the architecture of the global financial system and the meaning of monetary trust.

◆

The Digital Amero: A Strategic Framework for North American Finance

Exploring the Digital Amero as a policy thought experiment and strategic framework for understanding the future of North American monetary coordination in the digital age.

⇄

XRP and Cross-Border Payments: Lessons for Financial Infrastructure

Examining XRP and blockchain-based settlement as a case study in how digital payment rails may transform cross-border commerce, remittances, and liquidity management.

⚗

Stablecoin Regulation: Why the GENIUS Act Matters

A deep dive into the GENIUS Act, the CLARITY Act, and the evolving U.S. regulatory landscape for stablecoins and digital assets, and what it means for the future of finance.

$

What the Eurozone Can Teach North America

Lessons from the euro experience, including what worked, what failed, and what North America can learn as it considers the role of digital currencies in continental financial integration.
