The North American Monetary Union — Research Database

A curated reference for journalists, policymakers, students, and citizens. Foundational academic work, current policy data, and the technology stack behind the digital amero — all in one place, all free.

The amero is not a new idea. It was named and argued for in 1999 by Professor Herbert Grubel at the Fraser Institute, the same year the euro went virtual. A quarter-century later, the case has been transformed by blockchain, stablecoins, and the legislative breakthrough of the GENIUS Act. This database tracks both threads — the historical record and the live policy debate.
