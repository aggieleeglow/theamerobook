The Book

The Amero: Digital Bridge to the Fintech Future

By Che-Sun Lee

About the Book

A Serious Thought Experiment for the Digital Financial Era

The Amero: Digital Bridge to the Fintech Future examines the future of money through the lens of stablecoins, digital currency, monetary trust, blockchain infrastructure, and North American economic cooperation. Rather than presenting the Amero as a conspiracy theory, Che-Sun Lee frames it as a serious thought experiment about how Canada, the United States, and Mexico may need to think about financial infrastructure in a rapidly changing digital economy.

Key Themes

What the Book Covers

Across its chapters, The Amero explores the critical forces reshaping the global financial landscape:

  • Digital currency and programmable money
  • Stablecoin regulation and the GENIUS Act
  • XRP and cross-border settlement
  • North American monetary cooperation
  • Dollar reserve currency pressure
  • Blockchain-based financial infrastructure
Inside the Book

Chapter Overview

From the opening chapters on the history and theory of monetary unions to the final pages envisioning what North American financial architecture may look like between 2026 and 2032, The Amero takes readers on a structured journey through digital currency, stablecoin governance, cross-border payments, and geopolitical monetary competition.

Each chapter builds on the last, moving from foundational concepts to practical policy questions. Readers can expect clear explanations of blockchain infrastructure, an honest assessment of the challenges facing traditional monetary systems, and a forward-looking framework for understanding how digital finance may reshape trade, trust, and economic sovereignty across North America.
