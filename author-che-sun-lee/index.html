About Che-Sun Lee

Che-Sun Lee is a strategist, author, social entrepreneur, and blockchain education advocate focused on digital finance, monetary trust, and the future of economic systems.

Her work explores the intersection of international finance, technology, public policy, and economic transformation. In The Amero: Digital Bridge to the Fintech Future, Lee examines how stablecoins, blockchain infrastructure, cross-border payment systems, and regional monetary cooperation could reshape the financial future of North America.

Rather than treating the Amero as a conspiracy theory, Lee reframes it as a serious strategic question for the digital financial era: how can Canada, the United States, and Mexico prepare for a world shaped by programmable money, geopolitical competition, and changing trust in traditional monetary systems?

A graduate of Toronto Metropolitan University with a Bachelor of Technology, Lee brings a multidisciplinary perspective shaped by entrepreneurship, blockchain education, and public-facing innovation. Through The Amero, she invites policymakers, economists, fintech leaders, business professionals, and engaged citizens to consider what kind of financial architecture North America may need between 2026 and 2032.
